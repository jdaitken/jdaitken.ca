Pro: $900/month
Includes: everything in Growth, Google Ads managed for you (you pay Google directly for ad spend, pause anytime with no penalty), quarterly 15-minute strategy call, same-day response, priority on everything.

Billing options: monthly (default), annual prepay (12 months for the price of 11), or seasonal billing (same 12-month total, weighted heavier April-October and lighter December-March).

Caretaker: $75/month. Not a featured plan — offered directly to existing clients pausing active marketing, e.g. for the off-season, or to anyone who wants the site kept live and safe with no active growth work. Includes hosting/SSL/domain, security updates and backups, GBP kept claimed and monitored, contact forms and call tracking kept working. Website-only / one-time build: for businesses that just want a site with no ongoing marketing, JD Media also offers one-time project builds, priced as a custom quote. Not a featured plan — ask directly.

Ownership: during the plan, the site is a managed service (hosted and maintained by JD Media). Clients are never locked in.

Launch timeline: most sites go live in 7-14 days after content is received.
